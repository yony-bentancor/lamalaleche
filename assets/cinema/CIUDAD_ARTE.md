# Ciudad del nivel 3

Ilustración original generada con imagegen en modo generación de imagen nueva, sin imágenes de referencia. Archivo integrado: `ciudad-nivel-3.webp`. La botella, el movimiento, los subtítulos, las luces y el sonido se componen por código.

## Prompt usado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic background for the 2D hand-painted platform game La Mala Leche. Wide 16:9 detailed painted cartoon night city, no characters at all. A lonely empty sidewalk across the lower third, continuous perfectly horizontal walkable line at 73% height. On the far LEFT edge the outside of a supermarket with closed glass automatic doors and tiny magenta red neon lights; center and right an ENORMOUS city seen from sidewalk side view, tall moody blue and violet brick buildings, a distant avenue opening in the middle with atmospheric depth, wet asphalt, a few amber windows, subtle reflections, power cables and one streetlamp. At the far right an inviting but mysterious warm yellow-lit cafe doorway, no readable text. Moonlit sky, nighttime isolation and wonder rather than horror. Rich textured digital painting, clear dark illustrated outlines, polished indie 2D game aesthetic, steel blue and night violet dominant, restrained neon cyan and pink accent, warm light as possible destination. Not photorealistic, not flat vector, not pixel art. Keep lower walking band clear with dark blurred foreground below. City architecture feels huge relative to a small walking character that will be added by code. Landscape 2048x1152 if possible. No people, no animals, no vehicles in foreground, no lettering, no captions, no UI, no watermarks.
